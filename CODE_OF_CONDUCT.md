# Code of conduct

M&M Flow Lab repositories are professional research spaces. Participants must communicate respectfully, give constructive feedback, credit others' work, and avoid harassment or discrimination.

Concerns should be reported privately to the repository maintainers or to the University of Maryland through the appropriate institutional channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as circumstances allow.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect a constructive working environment.
