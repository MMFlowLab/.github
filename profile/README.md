# M&M Flow Lab

We study the mechanics of particulate and multiphase systems, with an emphasis on granular flows, suspensions, clogging, porous media, interfaces, erosion, and environmental transport.

Our group combines experiments, imaging, physical modeling, numerical methods, and data-driven analysis to understand how complex flows organize, fail, and transition between flowing and arrested states.

The M&M Flow Lab is based in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Maryland, College Park.

## Research areas

- Granular and suspension flows
- Clogging and intermittent transport
- Particles and fibers in porous media
- Cohesive and wet granular materials
- Erosion and geophysical flows
- Interfacial flows, impact, freezing, and melting
- Imaging and data-driven analysis of complex flows

## Using our repositories

Public repositories contain research code, documentation, and small example datasets associated with published work. Large experimental datasets and videos are deposited in archival data repositories and linked from the relevant project.

Please cite the associated paper and archived dataset when reusing material. Each repository provides its own license and citation information.
